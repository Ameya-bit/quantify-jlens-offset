# 0.2.0 — The suppression battery (step 5 complete)

*24 Aug 2026. Qwen3.5-4B throughout (the only model with a base/instruct
pair in the study). Ran because the step-4.0 gate passed; `src/h2_battery.py`
refuses to start otherwise (the pre-registered skip condition).*

## What step 5 asked

H2, as narrowed on 21 Aug (D30): tokens that instruct-tuning *inhibited*
(high Δ_t — the base model assigns them more probability than the tuned
model does) are over-represented among the tokens the lens over-reads by
default (high m_t), **over and above what their rarity predicts**. The last
clause does the work, because both sides of the comparison are
frequency-contaminated: Δ_t correlates +0.354 with log frequency (D23), and
step 4c showed mid-depth m_t does too. Every H2 statistic is therefore
computed both ways per the decision rule fixed in steps.md before any number
existed: raw, and residualised via the frequency-matched-control machinery
validated in `src/delta_t.py` (eligible pool of 92,846 ordinary measurable
tokens — asserted in-script, not re-derived; ±0.25 log-count window; probes
with <20 matched controls skipped).

Design: per (instrument × layer × K∈{100, 1000}), the top-K tokens by m_t
are probes; each matched probe's *excess* is its Δ_t minus the median Δ_t of
its frequency-matched controls; the statistic is the median excess, with an
(approximate — probes share controls) two-sided sign test at the pre-set
p < 0.01. All three instruments at all 31 layers, after the step-4 red-team
lesson that a J-only report can hide an opposite-sign logit result.
Corroboration: vocabulary-wide Spearman(m_t, Δ_t) raw and residualised
(D37 grid), partial Spearman controlling log f, and the base-model contrast.
Coverage: the pile-count frame cannot probe the junk (non-Latin tokens are
unseen in pile-10k), so a second wordfreq-matched frame was added in-step,
before its numbers were seen, with **within-script** cells (D36 — the first,
script-blind design manufactured excess out of a script-level Δ shift and
was corrected the same day, recorded there).

`src/h2_battery.py` → `results/step5_h2_battery.json` +
`results/step5_h2_excess_by_depth.png`.

## Finding 1 — H2 is NOT what the junk is made of

Two independent measurements, same direction:

- **The junk cell** (`wf_nonlatin`, K=1000: non-Latin probes vs non-Latin
  controls at matched wordfreq Zipf — the first time H2 touches its
  motivating tokens): early layers (L0–4) show **no suppression
  enrichment**. J-lens: −0.006 to −0.015, all non-significant. R-lens:
  *negative* and significant at L0–1 (−0.028, p = 2e-6; −0.026, p = 4e-5) —
  the junk the R-lens over-reads early is *instruct-favored* relative to
  comparable non-Latin tokens. Logit: +0.01 to +0.04, significant only at
  L4.
- **The script-level shift**: median Δ_t of wordfreq-covered non-Latin
  tokens is **−0.095** (tuning *promotes* them in English contexts) vs
  +0.002 for Latin. If the early CJK junk were suppressed content leaking
  through, this number should be positive. It is not.

Caveat carried by both (D36): Δ_t was measured on English contexts, so for
non-Latin tokens it reads "how much more the base model leaks toward these
in English text" — which is exactly the context where the junk phenomenon
appears, but is not a statement about Chinese text.

Related composition fact, free from step 4: the early top-m_t lists on this
model are `\xa0`/soft-hyphen/byte fragments/code debris — the NSFW-tokens
observation that motivated H2 on an earlier Qwen does not describe
Qwen3.5-4B's top offset tokens in the first place. Profanity's suppression
is real (+0.226 over matched controls, D24) but profane tokens are not in
the offset's top-K.

## Finding 2 — on the measurable (Latin) tokens: null on J, real-but-small on R, instrument-dependent

The registered test (early layers L0–4, pile frame, K=100), per instrument:

| instrument | raw gap | residualised excess | significant layers (p<0.01) |
|---|---|---|---|
| J | +0.009 | +0.021 | none (0/5; K=1000 also 0/5) |
| R | +0.099 | **+0.153** | L2, L3, L4 (K=1000: L0, L2, L4) |
| logit | +0.044 | **−0.089** | all 5, negative |

- **J-lens (headline instrument): NOT SUPPORTED.** Early excess is ≈0 both
  raw and residualised, at both K, in both frequency frames, and with
  capped-counts matching (robustness line: L0–4 all n.s.). The
  "residualising removes a mediator" caveat from steps.md cannot rescue it:
  the *raw* gap is also ≈0 (+0.009).
- **R-lens: a small, real early enrichment.** +0.15 median excess at K=100
  (L2–4 p ≤ 7e-4), +0.04–0.05 at K=1000 (p ≤ 2e-4), and it survives the
  frame swap (wf_latin: +0.10, p down to 7e-5). Composition (independent
  re-derivation, fresh code): the positive-excess probes are ` somew`,
  ` ;-`, `–and`, ` \xad`, `…but`, ` recieved`, ` seperate`, ` thems` —
  misspellings, truncated fragments, emoticon debris. **The D30 axis
  exactly: informal web-text style, not obscenity, and not the non-Latin
  junk.** The R-lens over-reads tuning-inhibited web debris early; the
  J-lens does not.
- **Logit lens: the frames disagree in sign** (pile: −0.089, all early
  layers significant; wf_latin: +0.13 at L0, p = 6e-8). Per the pre-set
  rule the residualised/pile result is primary, but the disagreement is
  reported at full prominence rather than resolved: whether the logit
  lens's early tokens look tuning-inhibited depends on which frequency you
  match on — the D31 phenomenon, now on Δ_t.

Decision rule outcomes: J and R "agree" (raw and residualised share sign
early) → raw is the headline, residualised the robustness line; logit
"disagrees" → residualised is primary.

## Finding 3 — mid-depth: the confound in action, plus a punctuation composition effect

Mid-depth (L5–28, K=100 band means) raw gaps are positive (J +0.059,
R +0.122) — and residualising collapses them (J −0.023, R +0.009). The raw
positive is the measured frequency confound doing exactly what D23
predicted: mid-depth m_t favors frequent tokens, and frequent tokens carry
inflated Δ_t.

The residual negative pockets (J L18: −0.137, p = 5e-7) are a composition
effect, not suppression: the control pool excludes punctuation (D24 rule),
so punctuation probes are compared to word controls, and tuning *promotes*
formatting tokens. Split (D38, in the JSON for every cell): at J L18, word
probes −0.064 vs punctuation probes −0.338. Word probes alone are ≈0 in the
pile frame (−0.06 to +0.04 across mid-depth) while wf_latin puts them at
+0.05 to +0.18 — source-dependent, unresolved, disclosed.

## Corroboration — the offset is at most weakly aligned with the suppression axis

- Vocabulary-wide partial Spearman(m_t, Δ_t | log f) never exceeds **0.12**
  anywhere: J peaks at +0.09 (L18), early J ≈ +0.03, logit goes negative
  late (−0.16 at L24). Whatever the offset is, the suppression axis
  explains almost none of it at the vocabulary scale.
- Base-model contrast: Spearman(m_t, mean logprob_base) and
  (m_t, mean logprob_instruct) are near-identical at every layer (largest
  gap ≈ 0.008; e.g. J L18: +0.532 vs +0.535). The offset does not
  preferentially channel what the *base* model wants — no "the lens reads
  the pre-tuning model" story survives this.
- At early J layers m_t anti-correlates with *both* models' preferences
  (L2: −0.26 both) — the early offset tracks what neither model wants,
  consistent with junk rather than suppressed content.

## Verdict against the registered H2

**NOT SUPPORTED as an explanation of the junk** — the junk cell is null or
sign-reversed, and the script-level Δ says tuning promotes, not
suppresses, the junk's token class. **NOT SUPPORTED on the J-lens** — the
headline instrument shows nothing early, raw or residualised, any frame,
either K. **A narrow, real residue survives on the R-lens**: early layers
over-read informal/misspelled web-debris tokens beyond what their rarity
predicts, robust across frequency frames — the D30 generalised axis, with
obscenity playing no visible role. The instrument-dependence (R yes, J no,
logit source-dependent) is itself a finding and feeds step 6: the effect
lives exactly where J and R disagree most (L0–4, pairwise top-10 overlap
0.35/10 at L0).

## Honest flags

- **The sign test is approximate** (probes share controls; excesses are not
  independent). Stated wherever a p is stated.
- **Top-100 membership is noisy** (split-half identity overlap 0.56–0.87,
  step 4.0) — hence the K=1000 dual-report; every early-layer claim above
  holds at both K or is not made.
- **Unmatched probes**: the pile frame matches only 47–84% of early top-100
  tokens (the rest are unseen in pile-10k — mostly the junk); the wordfreq
  junk cell exists to cover exactly those, and its per-layer matched-probe
  counts (n = 155–405 at K=1000) are in the JSON.
- **The registered test is the early-band pile-frame number**; everything
  else (wordfreq cells, mid-depth, correlations) is labeled corroboration
  or exploration, and the one in-step design change (D36) was made before
  its numbers were seen and corrected before any conclusion was drawn.

## What steps 6–7 inherit

- **Step 6 (H3):** the R-vs-J split is now sharpened: R carries an early
  web-debris suppression residue J lacks, at exactly the depths where the
  two transports disagree most. The swap battery should check whether the
  offset that *shrinks* under R (relative to J) is the frequency-flavored
  part, the Δ_t-flavored part, or neither.
- **Step 7:** nothing here blocks the score-space correction; Δ_t adds no
  reason to prefer a different correction target.
- The Δ_t machinery survives another campaign unchanged; the punctuation
  split (D38) should be reused by anything that probes top-K lists against
  the D24 pool.
