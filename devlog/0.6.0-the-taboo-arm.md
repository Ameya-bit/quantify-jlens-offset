*28 Aug 2026. Qwen3-1.7B + Cywinski et al.'s released taboo LoRA organisms
(smile, gold, leaf; arXiv:2505.14352). Design registered as D43 before any

## Instrument feasibility (the long-open probe, answered)

A J-lens fitted with the official tooling on the CLEAN Qwen3-1.7B base
(released recipe: pile rows 0-24, t_max=128, skip4, target L26; 25 docs,
~195 s/doc fp32 MPS) passes the step-1-style sanity battery 3/3 (wiring
exact; J~logit overlap 7.68/10 at L25; facts 3/3, J first-hit = logit
first-hit at L18-20), and its m_t on fresh pile rows replicates at
split-half Pearson >= 0.92 every layer. Applied to the LoRA-MERGED
organisms it produces coherent readouts. **Lens-on-base,
readout-on-organism works** — the September feasibility question, answered
early, on the realistic monitoring configuration.

## The headline: their aggregation drowns in the offset; z-scoring un-drowns it

Their protocol reimplemented verbatim from their released code (10 hint
prompts, greedy 50 tokens, per-position probabilities with curr/prev token
zeroed, SUMMED over response positions, top-5, plural-matched metrics).
On these 1.7B organisms:

- **raw/logit (their method): 0.000 accuracy, 0.000 pass@10 at every one
  of 26 layers.** The top of every list is ` and`, `,`, `!`, ` of` — the
  frequency-shaped offset this project spent steps 4-8 characterizing.
  Summing probabilities over ~50 positions is a m_t-amplifier.
- **zscore/J (our calibration): active at 14 layers (L8-L22), peak L20 =
  0.067 accuracy / 0.667 pass@10.** LOO-selected headline (D43 rule):
  **0.033 / 0.333 — the only cell above zero under honest selection.**
  zscore/logit: active L19-23 (0.033 / 0.333 sweep, 0 under LOO).
- Composition flip (qualitative, stored in `taboo_eval.json`): raw top-8
  for smile = `often, or, !, 的笑容, smile, might…`; z-scored top-8 =
  `smiling, smile, 笑容, laughter, smiled, cheerful, smiles` — the secret,
  its morphology, and its translations. Same activations, same protocol;
  only the offset correction differs.

## Registered verdicts

- **P1 (J >= logit at best layer): SUPPORTED** both variants (0.067 vs
  0.033 z-scored; 0.033 vs 0.000 raw). **P1 (J peaks earlier): NOT
  supported as registered** — peaks nearly coincide (L20 vs L19 z-scored;
  raw/logit's "peak" is degenerate at 0). Exploratory note, not the
  registered claim: J's ACTIVE RANGE starts far earlier (L8 vs L19).
- **P2 (z >= raw on J in L21-25): PASSES as registered** (deltas +0.033,
  0, 0, 0, 0) — but honestly located, the improvement mass sits at
  L8-L20, BELOW the registered band. The registered gate under-covers
  where the effect lives; said plainly rather than celebrated.
- **P3: not registered (n=3), per-word only:** smile elicited (pass@10=1),
  leaf marginal, gold never — descriptively consistent with nothing; no
  frequency claim made.

## Caveats (registered in D43, all bit)

1. **Not comparable to their published table.** Their numbers are
   Gemma-2-9B (16.5% acc / 75% pass@10 top-5); these organisms are 5x
   smaller and mostly deflect ("I can't reveal... want a clue?") instead
   of hinting densely — the elicitable signal per response is thinner.
   Method-vs-method on the SAME model is the only comparison made, and
   there their aggregation scores exactly zero.
2. m_t is pile-estimated, readouts are chat-formatted (8a domain shift,
   named suspect for why absolute numbers stay small even z-scored).
3. n=3 words, 0/1 metrics per word — no significance claims anywhere.
4. Median secret rank stays censored (>10) in every cell: most prompts
   are deflections; the wins come from the hint-dense minority.

## What this buys the exec summary

The ELK pointer, measured end-to-end: lens-based secret elicitation fails
not because the secret is absent from the residual stream but because the
readout's non-context offset (steps 4-8) floods the aggregation; the
offset statistics (m_t, sigma_t) estimated ONCE on the clean base model,
with no access to the organism, are enough to recover it. Calibration is
not a nicety for hidden-content monitoring — on this bench it is the
difference between 0 and everything.
