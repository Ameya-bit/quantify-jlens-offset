## Registered verdicts (LOO layer selection throughout)

- **Headline (LOO): calibrated J-lens 0.805 accuracy / 0.900 pass@10 vs
  their method (raw/logit) 0.665 / 1.000.** zscore/logit 0.715; raw/J
  0.545. Calibration adds +14 accuracy points over the best uncalibrated
  method under honest selection; raw/logit keeps the pass@10 crown (a
  sweep across 40 layers makes at-least-one-prompt hits easy).
- **P1 (J >= logit at best layer): FAILS raw (0.635 < 0.740, both L32),
  PASSES calibrated (0.815 > 0.745).** On the 9B the uncalibrated J-lens
  is WORSE than the logit lens — the transported instrument carries a
  bigger offset (step 6's R/J~2x lesson, now on J itself at scale) and
  only wins once the offset is standardized away. "J earlier" fails in
  both variants (peaks coincide).
- **P2 (z >= raw on J, late band L35-39): PASSES, decisively** — deltas
  +0.27/+0.38/+0.54/+0.46/+0.05, and this time the effect mass sits
  INSIDE the registered band (unlike the Qwen arm).
- **P3 (rho(zipf, LOO accuracy) > 0 on zscore/J): FAILS as registered.**
  rho = -0.20 (p = 0.40); every cell null (raw/logit +0.17 n.s.,
  zscore/logit -0.37 n.s.). Within this benchmark, secret-word frequency
  does not predict elicitation. Range restriction disclosed, not pleaded:
  all 20 taboo words are common English (zipf ~4.3-5.8), so the test has
  little leverage on the rare-token regime where the ELK concern lives —
  but the registered prediction was made on exactly this set and it did
  not hold. The 8d dose-response (damage from SUBTRACTING m_t scales with
  frequency) is a different quantity and stands unaffected.

## What this adds to the story

Same three-part structure as the Qwen arm, one scale up: the raw
aggregation is offset-dominated (raw/J loses to raw/logit because J's
offset is bigger); z-score calibration — built entirely from the CLEAN
base model, no organism access — turns the J-lens from the worst
instrument into the best one (0.545 -> 0.805). The correction transfers
across model families (Qwen3-1.7B, Gemma-2-9B) and across regimes (from
"unlocks anything at all" at 1.7B to "+14 points over the best method"
at 9B). What does NOT transfer: the frequency dose-response as an
elicitation predictor (P3 null) — the offset's frequency shape matters
for calibration damage (8d), not for which common secrets surface.
